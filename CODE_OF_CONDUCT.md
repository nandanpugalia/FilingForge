# Code of Conduct

FilingForge adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, as its Code of Conduct.

> _FilingForge is currently maintained by one person — references to "maintainers" below mean, for now, that single maintainer._

In short: **be respectful, welcoming, and considerate.** Demeaning comments, personal
attacks, and harassment of any kind have no place here — in issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages, or any other project space. We want FilingForge to be a
place where people of every background can contribute comfortably.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps the community:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward others
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from them

Unacceptable behaviour includes personal or political attacks, public or private
harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and any other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
maintainer. While the project is young, the confidential route is GitHub's
[**private advisory channel**](https://github.com/nandanpugalia/FilingForge/security/advisories/new)
— it's the available private inbox today (a dedicated conduct address will be added
as the project grows). All reports are reviewed and handled discreetly, and the
reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and will take fair, appropriate
corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate.
