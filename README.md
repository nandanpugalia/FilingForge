# FilingForge

> *A division of AlphaForge.* Turn any Indian company's official filings into a clean,
> **AI-ready library** — on your own computer.

**Status:** early development (private). Public launch later.

FilingForge downloads a company's official **BSE** filings (annual reports, results,
investor presentations), organises them per-company, converts them to clean Markdown, and
maintains an `INDEX.md` your AI reads first — with **smart incremental refresh** that pulls
only what's new.

- **Local-first** — runs on your machine, your files, no account, nothing leaves your computer.
- **One-click desktop app** (Windows + macOS), dark theme.
- **Open-core** — the engine is free + open; premium skill-packs come later.

Design, plans, and strategy live privately in the maintainer's vault during development.
